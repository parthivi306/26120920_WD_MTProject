## 💡 About the Design

Rather than building a standard corporate resume, I wanted this site to feel like a warm, editorial portfolio that reflects who I am and what I enjoy working on.

Here are a few design choices I made:
- **Warm Color Palette:** Instead of harsh plain white, I chose a warm cream background (`#FFFDF9`) paired with a rich burgundy (`#6E1F2E`) for headings and a terracotta accent (`#EA580C`) for buttons and badges.
- **Editorial Typography:** I paired **Playfair Display** (a classic serif) for headlines with **DM Sans** for clean, readable body text.
- **Thoughtful Spacing & Cards:** Projects like my **EY CAFTA Case Championship** submission and my ongoing participation in the **McKinsey Forward Program** are framed into interactive cards with subtle hover lift and shadows.
- **Responsive & Lightweight:** Pure HTML5 and vanilla CSS with no heavy libraries or frameworks, designed to load fast and look great on both laptops and phones.
